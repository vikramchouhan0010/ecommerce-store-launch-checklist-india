# E-commerce store launch checklist (India)

Work through this before you launch an online store, whether you hire an agency or build it yourself.

## Platform and scope
- [ ] Platform chosen (Shopify or WooCommerce) with a reason tied to catalogue size and integrations
- [ ] Written scope: pages, product count, variants, who uploads the catalogue
- [ ] Hosting, backups and security updates assigned to a named owner

## Payments, shipping, tax
- [ ] Payment gateway approved and tested with a small live order
- [ ] Shipping partners and rate rules set; pincode serviceability checked
- [ ] GST invoice format and tax settings checked with your accountant

## Pages that sell
- [ ] Clear product titles, photos and specifications on every product page
- [ ] Returns, shipping and contact pages written in plain language
- [ ] Mobile checkout tested on a real phone

## Search and tracking
- [ ] One category structure, readable URLs, no duplicate pages
- [ ] Product schema and a sitemap submitted to Google Search Console
- [ ] Purchase, add-to-cart and enquiry events tracked before any ad spend
